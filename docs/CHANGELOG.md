## 2026-11-08 (Round 7 gesture watchdog: R7-1, R7-3, R7-4, R7-5, R7-6, R7-8; no Dexie version)

- **Root causes.** (1) A pointer that is down when the app goes to the background (another app or tab, the Update reload) never gets its `pointerup`/`pointercancel`. Everything that remembered "a pointer is down" then stayed stuck for good: the ink engine kept a half gesture (`controller.gesture`) and refused every later Pen, Eraser and Lasso stroke (`down()` and `lassoDown()` return while a gesture exists); a Lasso or handle gesture left the page `contenteditable=false` (`holdEditing`), so taps and the caret failed; a grip drag kept `gripDragging()` true (ink and Lasso ignore every pointer) and the page read-only; the selection guard's `pointerDown` stayed true, so every late collapse counted as the person's own and the double-tap word vanished (R7-4); the palm guard and penTap kept a Pencil "down". (2) The ink engine waited at most 120 frames (2 s) for the editor's container; a reload right after an update fetches the editor's files over the network, and the page then had no ink engine at all (R7-1). (3) When a new service worker takes over a page that is already running (Update pressed in another window, or the plugin's own reload missed), the old code kept running on the new version's files; nothing restarted it. (4) Live sync's `restoreCaret` put back a caret, never a range, after a synced block arrived: a selected word a few seconds old was lost.
- **Fixes.** New `app/gestureReset.ts`: one watchdog bus. On `visibilitychange` (both ways), `pageshow`, `pagehide`, window `focus` and `blur` every registered piece forgets its transient gesture state: `InkController.resetGestures` (a long half stroke is kept as writing, a stray touch dropped; erase finished; pen-down, double-tap memory, finger tap, hidden set, the page's editable hold and every pointer capture released; the pad armed again), `gripDrag.resetGripDrags`, penTap, palmGuard, `ownSelection.pointerDown`, tapSelect's counter, holdSelect's press, penGuard's `penState.down`, `selectGesture`. The pad also listens to `lostpointercapture` (the browser taking a pointer away ends that gesture, R7-3). `InkSurface` waits for `.bn-container` with a MutationObserver (no time limit). `updates.ts` `watchControllerChange` + `reloadWhenSafe`: a new controller on an already-controlled page restarts it at a safe moment (showing, no pen down, nothing unsaved; at the latest after 20 s); the Update button also resets gestures before restarting. `ownSelection.KEEP_MS` is 30 s (the keep ends at the next tap or key anyway; a late collapse with no input is always put back). `liveApply` keeps the selection as a range (anchor and head by block and offset) and only puts it back when the change moved it.
- **Debug dump.** Settings › Advanced › "Tool state › Copy tool state" (this device only; `app/toolStateReport.ts`): app build, visibility and focus, the tool, page mode, service-worker state, editable counts, the ink pad, and every registered piece's state (ink gesture, pen-down, held captures, pad pointer-events and touch-action, editable hold, selection counts, layers, tap and cancel counters; grip drag; palm guard; penTap; own-selection flags; tap counter; hold press; pen guard).
- **Tests.** Unit: `app/gestureReset.test.ts`, `app/updateRestart.test.ts`, `app/tools/gripDrag.test.ts` (jsdom), `editor/liveApply.test.ts` (a selected word survives a synced change). Browser (`e2e/gestureWatchdog.spec.ts`, chromium and ipad-webkit): Pen, Eraser and Lasso after a lost lift while hidden; a Lasso loop left open (page editable again, next loop selects); a bfcache `pageshow`; eraser after Pen, Lasso and a half handle drag; double and triple taps after a lost lift with the Text tool on; a double-tap selection still there after 5 s with sync live-apply applying another device's block.
- **Files:** `src/app/{gestureReset,toolStateReport,updates,penTap,palmGuard}.ts`, `src/app/tools/gripDrag.ts`, `src/main.tsx`, `src/ink/{controller.ts,InkSurface.tsx}`, `src/editor/{ownSelection,tapSelect,holdSelect,penGuard,gesture,liveApply}.ts`, `src/settings/labSettings.ts`, `src/i18n/{enO,esO,hiO}.ts`, tests above, `playwright.config.ts`, docs.

## 2026-11-06 (Ink redraw: leave the canvases alone when nothing moved)

- **Problem:** `pencil-webkit` (ipad-webkit) failed `inkLayers.spec.ts` "500 strokes draws within the budget" on about half of the runs since a29ff83 (81 to 163 ms a frame against 80), even with the median of five rounds. The deploy of all of Wave 3 waited on it.
- **Cause:** every layout check (`dirty()`: a resize, any editor DOM mutation such as a keystroke, a ResizeObserver tick) ran a full `redraw`: three screen-sized canvases cleared, the layer bitmap put back, the three canvas layers (one with `mix-blend-mode: multiply`) composited again. The strokes themselves were already cached in a bitmap (no per-stroke work). In Chromium that frame is 1 to 3 ms; in CI's software-rendered WebKit, at the iPad's 2x canvases on a shared 2-core runner, it is about 80 to 130 ms, so the test always sat at the edge of its budget. A probe (branches `diag/ink-budget-head` and `diag/ink-budget-live`, Chromium) shows the per-frame work is identical at a29ff83 and at HEAD: 6 `getBoundingClientRect`, 3 `clearRect`, 1 `drawImage`, 0 stroke fills. So no Wave 3 code slowed the redraw; the budget was never safe in WebKit.
