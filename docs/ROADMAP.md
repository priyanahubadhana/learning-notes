- **Phase 8 — Deep research:** Claude Code runs it. Supabase + encrypted sync of block data, Safari/iPad PWA limits, Google Drive API limits, current free-plan catches, smart merge options.

Phone: usable. Check the parked WebKit test failures (run `npm run test:e2e:webkit`) on a real iPad. Page "⋯" menu from IDEAS. Fresh start is fine (mostly test data). At the end, a throwaway Google sign-in test on the real iPad (see RESEARCH.md); it decides the Drive part of Phase 10. Before closing, the pre-sync feature batches (owner, 2026-11-03; each batch goes live for the owner to try before the next, new screens get a mockup first): **1 Backup:** restore in place with a safety copy and Undo restore; two kinds, "Everything" as one zip (limit raised, about 1 GB tested on the iPad) and a quick "Notes only"; "share a notebook" stays separate. **2 Pencil test:** a throwaway page on the iPad decides whether web ink is good enough. **2c (done 2026-11-04):** iPad fixes, Toolbar menu, Auto switch, colour button, copy and paste options. **2g (done 2026-11-05):** the page ⋯ menu tidied (Page address ▸, Device mode ▸, Copy & paste ▸) and a Device mode button in the top bar. **2d (done 2026-11-04):** short bar with a Write · Draw · Read switch and per-set tools, control panel (search every feature), device picker, Recent, Favourites and Calendar in the sidebar. The full ribbon (File, Home, Insert, Draw, View, Review tabs, two behaviours, Ctrl+F1) is step 2 of it. The Phase 12 Calendar screen is built here already (month and week). **3 Lock and Reading mode (built 2026-11-05, owner-approved choices):** lock a page, notebook or database (choices in Settings, page lock by default); Reading mode as its own feature: eye-friendly themes, font, size, spacing, width, smooth or paged scrolling, line focus, progress, lock-to-reading, read aloud with word highlighting. **4 Fonts and Voice:** handwriting-style fonts (English and Hindi) in Settings and per page; Voice mode reads pages, notebooks, cards, books and mind maps aloud with the free device voice behind a swappable voice interface; spoken commands and speech-to-text when online, with a one-time privacy notice; every control works with the iPad's Voice Control and VoiceOver. **5 Pictures and mind maps:** resizable pictures; mind map block with themes. **6 Ink:** pen, highlighter, eraser, undo, favourite pens, palm rejection, paper styles, zoom, lasso, shapes, scribble-to-erase, ink over text, audio tied to handwriting; ink blocks get a label for search. **7 Reader:** PDF then EPUB (DRM-free), highlights saved to any page with a link back, side notebook, split view. Books and audio stay on the device until sync. **8:** a three-day trial, then Spanish and Hindi native review, then close.
- **Phase 10 — Sync:** Approved design (RESEARCH.md): notes on Supabase Free; pictures, backups and spare key in a visible Google Drive folder; the PC keeps a third copy; two swappable connectors. Step 1: make records safe for two devices (order keys with id tie-break and suffix; stable ids for workspace, journal, day and month pages and templates; card ids from their block; tag merge rule; `updatedAt` on every write; Delete forever and pictures). Keep the connector shaped as "add changes, read changes since X". Big things (ink, mind maps, audio, books, pictures, History copies) go to the owner's Google Drive and the device, never Supabase, behind the swappable storage connector so a global service can replace it later. History keeps 10 copies of text pages and 5 of ink, mind map and media pages. Try three-way text merge per block before Yjs. Lines deleted from a live page are erased after about 30 days. Header tidying S7 goes here. Before sharing or sync: outside pictures show only after a tap, and the app says notes on the device are not encrypted. Google sign-in limited to the owner's email; end-to-end encrypted, with per-user access rules. Rules per data type, not only blocks. Newest edit wins per block and the losing version goes to page History. Spare key in Drive locked with a passphrase, plus a printed recovery code. New-device flow. Planned first merge of PC and iPad data. Encrypted Drive backups when the app is open (about 7 daily + monthly). Delete forever also clears pictures and cloud copies. Clock skew. Pictures. Health-data privacy review.
- **Phase 11 — Summary · Notes · Review tabs:** at page, notebook and app level; must fit the existing notebook tabs. "Add to Summary" keeps lines linked. See IDEAS.
- **Phase 12 — Maths, new-page start, Calendar (built 2026-11-07 as master plan Phase N; owner checks pending; inline formulas, a ribbon button and Year view parked):** visual maths editor + LaTeX, loaded only when used; template list with "Study (Recommended)" first; Calendar Day / Week / Month.
- **Phase 13 — Polish pack:** power features and polish from IDEAS; one commit per item; at most 5–7 owner checks, each under 2 minutes.
- **Phase 14 — AI:** learning companion and handwriting to text (English first; Hindi needs a paid model). AI permissions: an "Allow AI" switch per notebook, off by default, all listed in Settings › Permissions; pages show their notebook's setting with a link. Notes stay encrypted when stored; only chosen text is sent. MCP through a local server on the PC reading its own copy; AI changes shown for approval with one-tap undo. Spoken quizzes, "what is missing" suggestions. Ask about cost first. iPad browsers can't capture the screen. Sending notes to AI leaves the encryption, so the owner must decide.
- **Phase 15 — Before global launch:** smart merge, passkeys, sign-up, App Store app (wrap this app with a free tool such as Capacitor, after sync; needs a Mac, $99/yr; thin wrappers can be rejected). Cost-model decision: users pay, or each brings their own storage. Own domain just before public launch (ask first: it costs money). Privacy policy, account deletion, and a GDPR, DPDP and app-store check. About 20 Safari smoke tests on every push if free GitHub minutes allow; otherwise weekly.

## Keep in mind

- Online first, offline as a fallback: notes, cards and reading always work offline; features that need the internet show "Needs internet" (owner, 2026-11-03).
- New tools stay hidden until needed: one button or menu item each, one Settings section each, advanced options under "More".
- Parked: Kindle, Apple Books, Google Play Books and Audible connections.

- Phase 8 result: [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md). Read it before planning Phases 9 and 10.
- Smart merge is a must before any global launch.
- Deep research comes before building sync.
- A custom domain later is a new address, so browser data does not follow it.
- The 5-year vision is parked.
