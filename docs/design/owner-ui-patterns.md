# Owner UI patterns: what the owner looks for (learned 11 Nov 2026 from the toolbar mock-ups)

Apply these to EVERY screen, together with docs/design/quality-standard.md.

1. **Never drop a feature.** Inventory the area first (file:line), then map every feature to its new home.
2. **Beginner = calm essentials; Professional = everything, purpose-driven.** Group by task, most-used first, rare ones one tap deeper.
3. **One pattern everywhere.** Essentials on a pill or row. A "More" button (sliders icon + label) at the FRONT, never lost at the end, opens one card: header, section tabs (same order logic), one pane.
4. **Nothing covers the content.**
   - Every floating bar has a clear minimise button with its own icon, collapsing to a visible accent chip (icon + label) high on the right edge.
   - Bars can be dragged and snap to edges.
5. **Visible affordances.**
   - A thin scroll track under any row that scrolls sideways.
   - Overflow arrows get their own slot and never overlap a control.
   - Labels or previews explain purpose ("what is this?" must never be a question).
6. **No duplicates.** One name and one place per option. Shortcuts only when deliberate.
7. **Live previews.** Style, border, size and layout changes show immediately on the object.
8. **Easy targets.**
   - ≥44 pt hit areas; Pencil-friendly handles with pointer capture.
   - Long-hold (with feedback) for moves.
   - Hold-to-unlock rings for locks.
9. **Switches for on/off.** Segmented controls for choices, sliders for amounts, swatches for colours.
10. **Clean management UIs.** Pick on top (visual tiles with mini previews), Manage below (rename inline, duplicate, reset, delete in danger red, Undo toast), ✓/✕ footer.
11. **Premium look.** The existing tokens, glass bars, accent underline, Lucide 1.6 stroke icons, calm motion (150 ms), dark and light.
12. **Questions before big mock-ups.** Recommended picks marked; plain words with small examples.
