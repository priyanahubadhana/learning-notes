# Ink: drawing on pages (shared ink engine)

Security rules: [rules/security.md](../../rules/security.md). Check them before building. Plan: [redesign-2026-11.md](../plans/redesign-2026-11.md) (step 3). Research: [inspiration-apps.md](../research/inspiration-apps.md) (ink section). The tools, pills and cards are in [toolbar.md](toolbar.md).

## Purpose

Write and draw by hand on any note page, with the Apple Pencil, a finger (when allowed) or a mouse, in the same page as typed text. One engine for notebooks now; the reader (books and PDFs, step 5) uses the same engine, tools and layers.

## How it works

- **Where the code is:** `src/ink/` (engine), `src/core/inkTypes.ts` and `inkService.ts` (data). The page mounts `InkSurface` (`ink/InkSurface.tsx`) over the editor. The controller (`ink/controller.ts`) owns pointer handling and canvases; pure parts (geometry, anchoring, eraser, shapes, layers, document, history) have no DOM and are unit-tested.
- **Tools:** the main bar's Pen, Highlighter, Eraser and Lasso. While any of them is selected a transparent "pad" over the page catches the pointer, so the text under the ink never reacts (owner, 7 Nov: no drag-selection, no block outline, no Scribble, no scroll from the Pencil). The pad does not close an open options pill (the page would jump). Under the Lasso a finger scrolls (the pad's `touch-action` allows panning) and a finger tap or a mouse click on the pad places the caret (so typing stays one tap away); a Pencil (or mouse) tap or drag is a loop. A finger draws a loop only when "Finger can draw" is Always (no Pencil at all). The ink overlay has `user-select: none` and no touch callout, every stylus `touchstart`/`touchmove` on it is cancelled (handles and boxes included), and the page is made not editable for the length of a lasso or handle gesture (iPadOS Scribble then has nothing to write in). The overlay follows the page when a pill opens or closes (a ResizeObserver on the scroller).
- **Pencil-on-text table (owner, 7 Nov):** Pen, Highlighter, Eraser: a tap draws, tiny taps are ignored (below), double and triple taps make no ink and select nothing. Lasso: a tap or drag starts a loop; a double tap does nothing. Typing mode (Text tool): the Pencil acts like a finger: a tap places the cursor (the "tap twice" setting still applies), a double tap selects the word, a triple tap the visual line on screen (one row of a wrapped paragraph: the caret positions at that row's left and right edges, R6-19), four taps the whole paragraph (`editor/tapSelect.ts`, the one tap counter: taps up to 450 ms apart and within 12 px add up; a press over 300 ms or a drift over 12 px is not a tap; the Text tool stays and the mini toolbar shows). **The selection stays** (R6-18): from the second tap on, the Pencil's pointerdown and stylus touch are cancelled, so iPadOS sends neither its own double-tap selection nor the tap's late synthetic click (whose mousedown collapsed ours), and the selection is kept (`ownSelection.ts` `keepSelection`, 1.5 s: a collapse with no new tap or key is put back; the mini bar ignores the taps that made it). A quick re-tap in the same block under "tap twice" is therefore a double tap; the caret-placing second tap comes after a pause. A press-and-hold then drag selects (`holdSelect.ts`), Scribble keeps working. `penSelect.ts` no longer places a caret or selects on pen down (it only works where the ink engine is not mounted).
- **Taps are not ink (`ink/tapFilter.ts`, tunable):** a stroke that lasted under `TAP_MS` (90 ms) and travelled under `TAP_PX` (3 px) leaves no ink; a press held longer is a deliberate dot. A mark under `SHORT_MS` (150 ms) and `SHORT_PX` (3 px) is remembered: a second pen-down within `DOUBLE_MS` (300 ms) and `DOUBLE_PX` (10 px) of it is a double tap, the first mark is taken back (no Undo step) and nothing is drawn. Counted in `controller.taps`.
- **Input:** pointer events, `getCoalescedEvents` for every sample, `getPredictedEvents` for the line's tip (drawn for the moment, never saved), a `desynchronized` low-latency canvas for the stroke being drawn (Settings: Pen settings › Reduce latency). Pressure of 0 at the start is replaced; pressure is rescaled to what the device really sends (as in the pencil test).
- **Palm rejection:** a finger is ignored while the Pencil is down and for 0.7 s after. "Finger can draw" (Pen settings): Automatic (a finger draws, unless in Pencil mode or a Pencil has been used, then it scrolls and zooms), Always, Never. A Pencil never scrolls the page (a stylus `touchstart` is cancelled); a finger scrolls natively.
- **Pens (perfect-freehand, MIT):** Fountain (pressure, flat nib), Ball (even), Brush (strong pressure, long taper), Pencil (tilt widens, 85 % opacity), Marker (even, flat ends, 75 %). Thickness, colour, sharpness (taper), pressure, tilt, stabilisation (streamline), flatness, thickness by speed, dashed or dotted (`ink/outline.ts`). All style values are saved with each stroke, so a pen's look never changes later.
- **Stroke model (`core/inkTypes.ts`):** raw points as flat numbers, six per point: x, y (in page widths from the anchor block's corner), pressure, tiltX, tiltY, t (ms since the stroke began); plus `at` (UTC ms start, the hook for replaying ink with audio), style, `bw` (page width in px when drawn), `shape`, `fill`, `drawn` (page width and text size when drawn, for "View as drawn" in the reader).
- **Anchoring:** the stroke belongs to the block under its first point (else the block just above, else the page). On every layout change the blocks are measured again (`ink/layout.ts`), so ink moves down when text is added above and scales with the page width. If the block is deleted, the place it was last seen is used (`fb` on the chunk).
- **Highlighter:** drawn behind the ink on its own canvas (`mix-blend-mode: multiply`; plain see-through in Night). With "Snap to text lines" and words under the stroke, no ink is made: the words get the highlight mark (`ink/textSnap.ts`; the nearest of six named colours), which moves and copies with the text. "Save to notes" then also keeps the passage in a top-level page "Highlights" of the same notebook (made when first needed). The **Highlight eraser** (Eraser with "Erase highlights only") only takes away highlight strokes and highlight marks it rubs.
- **Eraser:** Precise (splits strokes at the touched part), Standard (reaches 35 % further, drops specks), Whole stroke; a size of 8, 18 or 32 px (a ring shows it). One erase gesture is one Undo step.
- **Pen gestures (settings):** scribble over ink erases it (at least 4 sharp turns, a long path for its size; if nothing is under it the scribble stays as ink). A quick closed circle around ink selects it and switches to the Lasso (if nothing is inside it stays as ink).
- **Draw and hold:** keep the pen still for 0.6 s at the end of a stroke and it becomes a line, rectangle, circle or ellipse, triangle, or arrow (`ink/shapes.ts`). Snap shapes: lines to 0, 45 and 90 degrees (within 8), nearly square rectangles to squares, nearly round ellipses to circles. Fill shapes: closed shapes get a 20 % tint. Shapes are drawn as clean lines in the pen's colour and width.
- **Lasso (owner design, 7 Nov):** free-hand or rectangle (Lasso options). The bar's filter buttons (Ink, Pictures, Text) each toggle and are remembered (`lassoSettings`); the default is **ink only**. Ink: a tap on a stroke selects it; a loop selects strokes with at least 60 % of their points inside (`LASSO_INSIDE`); the words underneath never react. Pictures: the loop also selects the picture blocks mostly inside it. Text: a loop around part of a paragraph selects **just the words inside** (R6-17): the words whose centre lies in the loop, from the first to the last in reading order and across lines, as one text selection with the usual text menu (`editor/lassoWords.ts`, freeform or rectangle). Lines taken in whole (most of the words' middle line inside, `lassoMenu.ts` `mostlyInside`, or every word inside) select as the editor's own block selection instead; a whole line plus part of the next is a text range. Ink and text or pictures together: the mixed menu (Copy, Cut, Delete as one Undo, Screenshot, Make card, Deselect) and the handles; dragging or the handles move the ink only, and the menu says so. A tap on empty paper lets go of the selection. A box with four corner handles (resize, keeping the shape), a handle in the middle of each side that is at least 110 px long (stretch one way), a separate round handle (turn, clicks to quarter turns within 5 degrees) and dragging the box (move); two fingers on the box twist it. Strokes are moved to the block they now lie over. **Menu:** a row of icons (default Copy, Cut, Duplicate, Convert to text, Colour) and ⋯ More (Delete, Thickness thin/medium/thick, Make card, Add to Summary, Name this drawing, Paste when something was copied, Replay when the drawing was written during a recording, Copy as picture, Save as picture). Which actions sit in the row is chosen in the Lasso card ("Actions in the menu row", `ink.lasso.row` on this device). Copy and Cut keep the strokes in an in-app clipboard (and a PNG on the system clipboard); Paste lands them 16 px to the right and down. Convert to text needs AI (Phase P) and says so. Make card (drawing alone) copies the picture and opens the card tool; Add to Summary (drawing alone) needs a name first and adds it as a Summary line (`ink:<id>`).
- **Layers:** a page has at most two ink layers over the text ("Layer 1", "Layer 2"; master plan A2, `MAX_LAYERS = 2` in `ink/layers.ts`; pages saved earlier with more keep them and can merge the extras). The active layer is remembered per page; Merge warns first and can be undone. Card: Pen, Highlighter or Lasso › Layers, or the ribbon's "Ink layers". Each layer: name, show or hide, lock, move up or down, select all, delete (Undo brings it back), which layer new ink goes to. New ink skips a hidden or locked layer. Merge opens up a locked or hidden target layer (Undo restores it); the eraser works on the active layer, or on every open layer when the active one is locked or hidden. The Highlight eraser clears text highlights live while rubbing. "Paper behind the ink": blank, lined, grid, dots (saved with the page); the spacing follows the text line height (`ink/paperGrid.ts`). "View as drawn" is shown dimmed (the reader, step 5).
- **Speed (9 Nov, ink scrolls natively):** the ink overlay is a child of a zero-size anchor at the end of the editor's container, so it sits inside the page scroller and the browser scrolls it with the text: scrolling runs no ink script (no measuring, re-placing, clipping or repainting), and the overlay can never land over the bars (the scroller clips it; this also ended the iPad drift on overscroll, so the bounce tracker and the visual-viewport listeners are gone). The finished ink is cut into **tiles** of 640 px (`ink/render.ts`, `TILE_H`): a tile has an `ink` canvas (every visible layer, in layer order) and, if it has highlights behind the ink, an `hl` canvas, only when strokes touch it. A page keeps at most 8 tile slots (`MAX_SLOTS`), reused by moving them with a transform (nothing is built or removed while scrolling), and gives spare canvases back when idle. Tiles near the screen (one tile above and below) are chosen again only after 160 px of scrolling (`SCROLL_STEP`) from cached geometry, and once with fresh geometry 150 ms after the scroll stops. One `live` canvas the size of the screen holds only what moves (stroke being drawn, eraser ring, lasso, peeled tape) and follows the screen while live. A tile is repainted only when its strokes, the layout, the width, the pixel ratio or the look changed. A zoomed paper (CSS `zoom`) scales the overlay's own px, so the overlay is zoomed back by the measured ratio (screen px = overlay px at every zoom). Test: `e2e/inkScrollNative.spec.ts`; numbers in [perf-big-pages-2026-11.md](../research/perf-big-pages-2026-11.md).
- **Undo and Redo:** one history with the text. Ink steps (a stroke, an erase gesture, a lasso change, a layer change) are kept with the text editor's undo depth; Undo takes the newest of the two (`ink/inkHistory.ts`, `editor/historyActions.ts`). Ctrl or Cmd+Z, the bar and the Undo toast all go through it.
- **Storage:** Dexie version 13 adds `inkLayers` and `inkChunks` (indexed by page, and layer). A chunk holds up to 60 strokes of one layer over one block; a save rewrites only changed chunks, 0.4 s after the last change and on hiding the page. Pages without ink have no rows. Empty chunks are soft-deleted.
- **Books (C2b):** the same engine runs over PDF pages and EPUB paragraphs in the reader (`BookInk`, a `BookSurface` gives the engine its blocks and unit; no text editor, so the Lasso picks ink only). See features/library.md.
- **History, backups, Trash:** a History copy of a page also holds its ink (restoring brings it back; ink changes alone make a copy). "Everything" and "Notes only" files both contain ink (it is note content, not media). "Add as copies" and notebook or page sharing do not carry ink yet (step 10). Delete forever erases the strokes and leaves empty markers. Replace accepts files from database version 12 (only tables were added in 13).
- **Accessibility:** the ink region is a group "Drawing on this page. Strokes: N". A keyboard door (first Tab stop inside the page area when strokes exist): arrow keys choose a stroke, Delete removes, Escape lets go; a status line names the stroke. Layers and the menu use 44 px buttons with names.

## Shapes, Tape, Ruler, Magnifier (G1, wave 3)

All four work on the ink layer, like the Pen; Lasso can select, move, scale and turn what they make (they are ordinary strokes).

- **Shapes:** the Pen has a "Draw as" row in its card (`PenSettings.drawAs`: Freehand, Line, Arrow, Box, Oval, Triangle, Tape). With a shape chosen, a drag from corner to corner draws the clean shape live (`ink/shapes.ts` `shapeFromDrag`; "Snap shapes" in Pen settings snaps lines to 45 degrees and near-square boxes and near-round ovals; "Fill closed shapes" applies). The ribbon's Shapes tool and Insert › Shape select the Pen and open that card. Choosing a pen type from the ribbon puts it back to Freehand. Draw-and-hold snapping still works in Freehand.
- **Tape:** a solid box (`Stroke.kind = 'tape'`, shape `rect`, fill, colour `TAPE_COLOUR`) that hides what is under it, ink and text. A **tap** on a tape (Pen or Tape, not a drag) peels it off to show what is under it, leaving a dashed outline; a second tap sticks it back. Peeled state is kept only while the page is open (not saved, no Undo). The Eraser takes a whole tape away with one touch inside it. Backup checks accept the new kind.
- **Ruler (7 Nov):** Insert › Ruler (or the ribbon) shows an 80 px bar over the page. The bar is a solid object: one finger or the Pencil anywhere on it moves it at once, a second finger twists it (the angle shows in the middle), both end handles turn and stretch it; turning clicks to every 15 degrees when within 2. Nothing on the bar reaches the text under it. A pen or highlighter that starts within 24 px of a long edge slides along that edge, so the line is straight (`ink/ruler.ts`, `controller.ts`). Not saved.
- **Magnifier (7 Nov):** Insert › Magnifier shows a pad across the bottom of the screen (30 % of it; drag its top edge, 20 to 50 %) with 2×, 2.5× (default), 3×, 4×, Space, Next line and close, and a target box on the page with a label ("Writing lands here · drag to move") that drags it. Writing in the pad (finger, Pencil or mouse) lands, small, in the box as ordinary ink, one Undo per stroke; the pen's width is scaled down with it. The pad shows the page's own ink inside the box (`controller.paintRegion`, redrawn on every ink change), so page and pad are always the same: erasing on the page clears the pad at once, and with the Eraser tool rubbing in the pad erases on the page (`beginErase`, `eraseMove`, `endErase`). When the pen lifts in the pad's right fifth the box moves on by 60 % of its width; past the right margin (5 %) it goes to the next line at the left margin with 10 % overlap. A hint explains the pad until the first stroke (`ink.magnifier.hinted` on this device). The pad's size is measured from the element itself (the old fixed size drew the ink to the right of the pen).
- **Lazy loading:** the renderer already paints only the part of the page on screen. Loading the strokes of a page chunk by chunk is not built (see DECISIONS: the plan's own rule was to add it only if a test shows a need; none did).
- **Not built here:** shape handles to adjust a drawn shape (Lasso resizes and turns it), Make card from a tape (the hidden area as a picture answer; E2's picture answers are the base), labels and Tape on pictures (G0 notes).

## Drawing labels (G3, wave 4)

- **Name this drawing** (Lasso menu, the T button) types a name (80 characters at most) onto every selected stroke (`Stroke.label`, an optional field: no Dexie version; validated in `checkRows.ts`; kept by move, scale, colour, duplicate and erase pieces because strokes are copied whole). Saving is one Undo step (`InkController.labelSelection`); an empty name takes it away. The box is prefilled when the selection shares one name.
- **Search:** `search/indexMiddleware.ts` also watches `inkChunks`: after any write (from ink, History restore, sync or import) the page's single entry `ink:<pageId>` (kind `ink`, score 150, listed as a note) is made again from all live chunks of the page, or removed when no label is left. Trashing or restoring the page flags/unflags the entry. A result row shows the labels as its text and opens the page. Not rebuilt by `ensureSearchIndex` (no labels exist before this feature; labels that arrive by sync pass the middleware).

## Gesture watchdog (Round 7, 8 Nov)

- **Why:** on the iPad a pointer that is down when the app goes to the background never gets its lift. Every "pointer is down" memory then stayed stuck until a restart: the engine's half gesture (no Pen, Eraser or Lasso after it), the page's "not editable during a Lasso gesture" hold (no taps, no caret), a grip drag (ink ignores every pointer), the palm guard, penTap, and the selection guard's pointer-down flag (the double-tap word vanished, R7-4).
- **What:** `app/gestureReset.ts` is one bus. On `visibilitychange` (both ways), `pageshow`, `pagehide`, window `focus` and `blur` every registered piece forgets its transient state. The engine's `resetGestures` keeps a long half stroke as writing (3+ points over 50 ms, the pointercancel rule) and drops a stray one, finishes an erase, releases the editable hold and every pointer capture, clears pen-down, the double-tap memory and the finger tap, and arms the pad again (`syncPad`). The pad also ends a gesture on `lostpointercapture`. `InkSurface` waits for the editor's container with a MutationObserver (no time limit: after an update the editor's chunk comes over the network). `app/updates.ts` restarts the page at a safe moment when a new service worker takes over a page that was already controlled (Update pressed in another window), so old code never runs on new files.
- **Seeing it:** Settings › Advanced › Tool state › Copy tool state (device only) copies every piece's flags (`InkController.debugState`, grip drag, palm guard, penTap, own selection, tap counter, hold press, pen guard) plus the tool, page mode and service-worker state as text.
- **Tests:** `e2e/gestureWatchdog.spec.ts` (chromium and the iPad stand-in), `app/gestureReset.test.ts`, `app/updateRestart.test.ts`, `app/tools/gripDrag.test.ts`.

## Edge cases

- Read-only pages (open in another window) and Reading mode show ink but do not draw.
- A stroke started near the bottom makes more room: the page is kept 240 px taller than its lowest stroke, and at least 60 % of the screen tall while an ink tool is selected.
- Very long pages: only the tiles near the screen are painted; all strokes of the page are loaded (not chunk by chunk yet).
- A damaged saved style or stroke in a backup is refused with the rest of the file (`share/checkRows.ts`).
- Two windows on one page: only one edits (existing page lock).

## Security check

Ink is device-local data. Every row from a file is checked field by field (stroke points must be whole groups of six numbers, at most 400,000 numbers per stroke, 1,000 strokes per chunk). No network. The only new dependency is perfect-freehand 1.2.3: MIT licence, no dependencies, 4.5 KB source, no production audit findings, used by one import (tree-shaken).

## Ink rules on paper (owner, final, 8 Nov 2026)

Each rule gets its own e2e test (chromium and ipad-webkit).

1. **Layers:** text blocks are the bottom layer and ink is a top layer. The Pen, highlighter and eraser change only ink.
2. **Linked to a line:** each stroke is linked to the text line under it (today's paragraph anchoring) and moves with it.
3. **Text tool:** a tap on empty space creates a new block or box there.
4. **Pencil on empty paper:** a new box is created under the ink, with an empty line, so the ink is always linked.
5. **Lasso, ink only:** the moved ink unlinks from its old line and re-links to the line under its drop spot. If there is no text there, an empty block is created to hold the link.
6. **Lasso, ink and text together:** both go to the new block as one piece, and the ink stays linked to that text.
7. **Deleting a line with linked ink:** the ink moves to the next line. Nothing is ever deleted silently.
8. **Link mark:** while ink is Lasso-selected, a thin, faint line shows the text line it is linked to.
9. **Copy and paste of a line** also copies its linked ink.
10. **Big drawings** that span many lines link to their top line and move as one piece.

All ten are built (8 Nov). Rule 5: ink dropped outside its box goes to the box there or a new box with lines (`ink/inkTransfer.ts`). Rule 6: a mixed Lasso drag moves the whole lines within the box; strokes are re-linked to the nearest selected line first (no undo step), so one Undo restores both. Rule 9: copy/cut of lines whose words are selected in full keeps their ink in the app; a paste whose text matches links it to the pasted lines (new lines in order, or the caret line), through the editor's copy/cut/paste events and the app's Copy, Cut and Paste as plain text. Rule 6 across boxes (9 Nov): dropped on another box or bare paper, the lines and ink go together (`moveLinesElsewhere`, `CarriedLines` in `inkTransfer.ts`). Tests: `e2e/paperInk.spec.ts` (chromium, ipad-webkit).

## Tools robustness (10 Nov)

Plan: [../plans/tools-robustness-plan.md](../plans/tools-robustness-plan.md). Research: [../research/tools-robustness-2026-11.md](../research/tools-robustness-2026-11.md).

- **One input machine** (`src/app/inputMachine.ts`): the Lasso, handle drags, Pencil text drag, hold-select, selection guards, grip drag and the auto device mode counter call `begin`. A lift, cancel, lost capture, app switch, tool change or **4 s without movement** drops the gesture through one function and resets its owner. The Lasso never returns early on a stale gesture (same pointer after a pause, or one the machine dropped).
- **One owner of contenteditable** (`src/editor/editLock.ts`): `lock(owner, el)` / `unlock(owner)`, counted; the page follows `editableIn(pageMode)`, nothing writes back a remembered value.
- **Health check** (`src/app/healthCheck.ts`): on visible, pageshow, focus and every 10 s when idle for 30 s. Heals stuck flags, locks, a wrong contenteditable, blank ink (repaint) and listener leaks; logged in Copy tool state under `health`. A heal failing twice within 60 s asks for a soft refresh.
- **Soft refresh** (`src/app/softRefresh.ts`): "Refresh app" in the settings gear menu, and the automatic last resort (safe moment only, at most once per 10 minutes, toast). Page, scroll and tool come back.
- Tests: e2e/{tools-machine,lassoLoops,toolsEditLock,toolsFlags,toolsHealth,toolsRefresh,resume}.spec.ts (all in the ipad-webkit list); test hooks on `window.__lnTools`.
