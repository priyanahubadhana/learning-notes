# P1 speed: "laggy and sluggish while switching" (owner, iPad, 17 Nov 2026)

Measured by Opus on slice/p1-speed. Method: the production build (E2E preview), Playwright, iPad size (1180 × 820 sideways, 820 × 1180 upright), Pencil device mode, the test preset's switches plus `textbox.v2`. A notebook of 20 pages, each with 10 boxes of two lines and 3 lines of main text. Time = from the tap (pointer-down time stamp) to the frame drawn after the expected change is on screen (median of 3 runs; page switch: 9). Chrome with the CPU slowed 4× (CDP `Emulation.setCPUThrottlingRate`, an iPad-like speed); WebKit (the iPad stand-in) at full desktop speed. Scripts: the session's scratch folder (measure.cjs, probe.js); the regression guard is e2e/p1-speed.spec.ts.

## Top causes found

1. **Opening a page of boxes waited seconds on WebKit (the iPad's engine).** Each box has its own editor, and each one ran the page's preparation by itself before showing: apply kept changes, then check card records in a _write_ transaction over 8 tables. 11 write transactions in a row, ~270 ms each on WebKit = ~4 s with the page on screen but no editors. Fixed: one shared preparation per page, and the card check only reads unless a card is missing (WebKit page switch 4.5 s → 0.9 s in the transaction probe).
2. **Every tool switch rebuilt every box's editor.** BlockNoteView rebuilds its whole ProseMirror view when its `editable` prop changes; the app changed it on each Text ↔ Pen switch (23 of 23 blocks redrawn). Fixed: the prop keeps its first value; the app's mode policy switches editing in place (0 of 23 redrawn).
3. **Layout thrash: the page was re-styled once per box.** Box frames measured themselves right after their editors had changed the page; each ink surface measured its place and looked up its scroller between other writes; the keyboard inset (window size) was read on every editor render; the shared scroll bar measured on every small change while a page was being built; the scroll hints rewrote unchanged attributes during a scan. Fixed: these reads are now done once per frame for all boxes, after the writes (Chrome page switch: forced style passes from ink, box frames and scroll bar 300+ ms → ~0 at full speed).

## Left (measured, not fixed here)

- **Chrome only: `:has()` rules make each editor switch re-style the whole page** (6 ms per box at full speed). It needs both our `body:has([data-ink-pad])` rules and BlockNote's own `.bn-suggestion-node:has(...)` rules; removing all `:has()` rules takes the Text/Pen switch from ~98 to ~29 ms on Chrome, while WebKit is unchanged (86 vs 82 ms). Because tool switches no longer rebuild the editors, this cost now shows on Chrome's Text switch (296 → ~530 ms at 4×, ~85 → ~130 ms at full speed); on WebKit Text got faster. Fixing it means changing BlockNote's style sheet (build step) and our three `body:has` rules: a separate task.
- **Chrome only: BlockNote reads the selection once per box while creating an editor** (`chromeKludge` in ProseMirror), ~6 ms per box at full speed on a page switch.
- **Box editors per page:** a page with 10 boxes still makes 11 full editors on open (~100 ms of editor setup at full speed). Lighter boxes (one editor, or read-only text until tapped) would be a design change.
