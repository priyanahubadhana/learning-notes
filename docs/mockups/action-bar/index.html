Action bar, PC

Redraw of 24 Nov 2026 (after the owner's first look). Icons are Lucide, 1.75 stroke, no fills except the filled pin. Every button that opens a picker or list carries ▾; a button that acts at once has none. Paste is one split button. Gaps follow a 4-pt grid and every target is 44 px (the Paste ▾ half draws 28 px wide and grows its hit area to 44). The bar is one row. Waiting for the owner's yes.

Keyboard (AB-09)

  • Reach the bar: Alt+F10, then ← → move along it, Enter presses (key: open point), Esc closes ⋯, then ends the selection.
  • Cut Ctrl+X, Copy Ctrl+C, Paste Ctrl+V, Delete Delete.
  • Move, Pin, Edit text, B/I ▾, Colour, Size, Font, Highlight, Align ▾, List ▾, Box style: key: open point (reached by Alt+F10 and the arrows only).
  • ⋯▾ and its list (Link, Duplicate, Lock box, Make card, Add to summary, Revise mark, Customise bar…): key: open point (reached by Alt+F10 and the arrows; Esc closes).
  • Paste ▾ format list: Esc closes it without pasting; a screen reader hears the list and the format.
  • Round pin button (folded): Enter or a click opens the bar; it drags like the bar; "Keep bar open" in ⋯▾ stops the folding.
  • Hold menu: right-click on the PC (S11).
