One bar for a selected box

Mock-up for the owner's OK. Not built yet. No stored data changes.

Small pop-ups (strips, colours, Paste, Box style, ⋯) open right under their button and may cover words while open; the bar itself never does. Each pop-up closes with a tap outside or its ✕, and always stays inside the page frame.

We will prove (plain words)

  1. Tap a box: one bar shows above it, with colour, size, font, B/I, align, list, fill, Cut, Paste, Delete and ⋯.
  2. The old box bar and the old text bar are gone: no second bar anywhere.
  3. Each button opens its small strip right under it, and closes with a tap outside or ✕.
  4. Paste ▾ offers Keep look, Match style and Plain text; a plain tap pastes the way Settings says.
  5. Box style has Presets and Advanced, every option of today's card is still there; changes show at once and one Undo reverses each.
  6. ⋯ keeps every old item (Copy, Duplicate, Box style, Move up/down, Group, Delete) and adds Lock box; a locked box cannot be moved, resized or deleted.
  7. Move the bar and pin it: it stays where I put it, on every box, after a reload.
  8. Box near the page top, near either edge, iPad turned, phone: the bar finds a free side and stays fully inside the page.
  9. Phone: a shorter bar; the rest is under ⋯; every target is 44 pt or more.
  10. Light and dark both readable; the same words in Beginner and Professional.

Must never happen

  • The bar never sits inside the box over the words.
  • The bar never goes past the page frame (off the paper or off the screen).
  • No feature of today's box bar, Box style card or ⋯ list is lost.
  • No typing, selecting or drawing by accident when I tap the bar.
