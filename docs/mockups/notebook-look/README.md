- **Look A** follows the owner's reference photos of medical study notes: dotted paper, a hand-lettered title, highlighter headings, small boxes in two columns, a sticky note, coloured diagrams with labels.
- **Look B** is the design system's own clean look: plain paper, little colour, callouts where A uses a sticky note.

## The colours and the fonts

- Highlight colours, each with one meaning: purple = a section heading (look A only); blue = a key term; pink = a limit (a number); green = a rule to follow. They are the app's own highlight colours. The sticky note uses the app's sticky yellow.
- Fonts: the app's own font files. Look A: Caveat Bold for the titles, Patrick Hand ("Handwritten") for the text. Look B: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next ("Easy to read").

## What the pictures show that the app cannot hold today

- The app's highlight colours are much paler than the highlighter in the reference photos, so look A is softer than the photos.
- The app's callout has no edge and is almost invisible on white paper; a thin border was added in look B.
- The diagrams (the flow of "what loads when", the folder icons) would be pictures. The app has no flowchart block.
- The mind map here uses the page's font and a larger text size than the app's mind map draws today.
- The "Map" column of the chapter table and the look of a fill-the-gap card are guesses.
- The progress on the front page (chapter 1 read, 2 cards) is sample data.
