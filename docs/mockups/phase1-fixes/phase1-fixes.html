1

The side list gets its own bar and moves the page aside

T4

On an upright iPad the side list opens with its own top bar (title and close), and the page's top bar and tool bar slide over to make room instead of hiding under it.

  • Upright: list 300 px wide next to the rail; the page column narrows and reflows. Tools that no longer fit go into the tool bar's ⋯ (never cut in half).
  • Sideways: the list stays docked as today, now with the same header bar, so both sizes look alike.
  • Phone: no room to move the page, so the list is a drawer with the same header bar; the page waits dimmed behind it.
  • Also fixed from your picture: the list's title is never scrolled under the top edge, and "November 2026" shows once.
2

Right-side sheets never cover the top bar

T4T21T22

Page options, Pin an action and every other right-side sheet now start just below the top bar, so your page title, Undo, Redo and search always stay in view. (Undo and Redo now sit at the top left, where back and forward were; the page path and the iPad swipe back still go back.)

  • The sheet keeps one fixed size (12 px from the edges, 440 px wide); only its list scrolls.
  • Only the page area below the top bar is dimmed. The first tap outside closes the sheet and does nothing else.
3

Page options: a few main actions, the rest folded

T22

Four main actions sit on top as tiles; Share, Protect and Tools are folded behind ▾ and open with one tap, like the tool bar ⋯ menu you approved.

  • Nothing dropped: Export, Lock this page, History and Search all features moved up as tiles; the rest stay in their group (Copy & paste, Paste as plain text, Lock editing, Pause reviews, Review this page, Prompts and skills).
  • Each folded group says what is inside in one grey line. Groups open folded every time the sheet opens.
  • Lock editing and Pause reviews are on/off things, so they show a switch with their current state.
4

Pin an action: ✕ and ✓ at the top

T21

Pick what to pin, then tap ✓ to keep it or ✕ to leave without changes; both sit at the top, far apart, so you can't hit the wrong one.

  • Nothing changes on your bar until you tap ✓. ✕, a tap outside or Esc keep the bar as it was.
  • The list is the same as today (you said you love it); only the ✓ and the round tick on each row are new.
5

Must-have update screens

P1-3T1T2T3

The five steps of a must-have update, with an honest time left, a "slow connection" note only when it is really slow, and a way back to your notes if it fails.

  • Time left: shown only after 3 seconds of measuring, from the speed of the last 5 seconds, rounded up ("about 10 seconds", "about 2 minutes"). It never goes up and down by more than one step. Before that it says "Working out the time…".
  • Slow note: only when the speed stays under 50 KB/s for 8 seconds in a row; it goes away by itself when the speed is back.
  • Still blocked (as update-v2 says): "Not now" during the countdown and "Cancel" during the download lead to Paused, with "Update now"; the app stays behind it.
  • New way out: when the update did not finish, nothing was installed yet, so "Not now" takes you back to your notes. A small pill offers "Try again", and it tries by itself on the next start. Only exception: if another tab already moved your notes to the new way of storing (U11), there is no "Not now", because the old version could damage them.
  • Offline stays as decided: never blocked; a small note at the bottom, and it continues by itself when you are back online.
