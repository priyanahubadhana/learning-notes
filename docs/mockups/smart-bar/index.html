We will prove (plain words)

  1. Put the cursor in a box: one bar shows Paste ▾ and the text buttons, no Cut, Copy or Delete.
  2. Select "2000": Cut, Copy and Delete join at the end (Link is under ⋯). The bar does not move or change into another bar.
  3. Select a box, a drawing, a picture or a mix: the same bar, in the same look, shows the buttons for that thing.
  4. Study preset: Highlight, Colour and Make card come first.
  5. Hold a button: its name shows. Delete is the only red button.
  6. The bar never covers the selected words and stays fully on the screen; it goes below the selection first, then above, then upright on the right, then on the left, then near the top tool bar; it moves, pins and docks as approved on 18 Nov.
  7. Phone: the same presets, fewer buttons, the rest under ⋯.
  8. Light and dark both readable; the same words in Beginner and Professional.

Must never happen

  • A button of the three old bars is missing (SB-N1).
  • A button that does nothing is shown (SB-N2).
  • The bar covers the selected words or goes off the page or screen (SB-N3).
  • The bar jumps or swaps to a different bar when words get selected (SB-N4).
  • The bar appears or moves while writing with Scribble (SB-N5).
  • Two bars for one selection, or Apple's bar in its place (SB-N6, SB-N7).
