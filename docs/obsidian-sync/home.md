# Obsidian Sync (personal)

**What this is.** "Obsidian Sync" is a private Google Cloud app that one person uses to keep their own Obsidian notes the same on their own computer and tablet, with a copy in their own Google Drive. It is used with the community Obsidian plugin GSync. It is not a public service and is not offered to anyone else.

**What it does.** When its owner signs in, the GSync plugin copies the owner's notes between the notes folder on the owner's devices and one folder in the owner's own Google Drive. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
