# Obsidian Sync (personal): privacy

Last change: 27 November 2026.

- **Who uses it.** Only its owner, with their own Google account. It is not offered to the public.
- **What data it touches.** The owner's own Obsidian notes and the files in one folder of the owner's own Google Drive, through Google's Drive API.
- **Where the data goes.** Only between the owner's own devices and the owner's own Google Drive. The app has no server of its own and keeps no copy anywhere else.
- **What is stored on the device.** The sign-in token that Google issues is stored by the GSync plugin inside the owner's notes folder on the owner's devices, so the owner does not have to sign in every time.
- **Sharing.** No data is sold, shared or shown to anyone else, and none is used for advertising or for training AI models.
- **Ending it.** The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the Google Cloud project.
