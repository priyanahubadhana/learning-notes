# The builder and tester lenses on the first three specs (20 Nov 2026)

Two Sonnet helpers read only the story and the rules of docs/features/lasso.md, text-select.md and selection-bar.md (not the scenarios) and gave their own view. Code line: `build/next` f65b1fa6. **V** = verified in code, **I** = inferred. This digest is the input for Opus when it writes each part's detail lines; the specs and the plan already carry the conclusions.

## 1. The Lasso and a resting hand (builder, V unless marked)

- **No palm guard on any Lasso path.** The 700 ms rule lives in `accepts()` (src/ink/controller.ts:1243), called only from `down()`, which returns early for the Lasso (:1262). `lastPenAt` and `penDown` are never updated under the Lasso, so a guard needs its own bookkeeping in `lassoDown`.
- **A palm can twist a selection.** While the Pencil moves a selection, any touch becomes the second finger of a twist (:2126-2139); there is no check that it is a real finger.
- **A palm can place the typing cursor in the middle of a loop.** A touch with no finger-lasso sets `fingerTap` (:2147-2158); on its lift `lassoUp` places the caret when it moved less than 8 px (:2241-2248), and that check comes before the gesture check.
- **With "Finger can draw: Always"** a palm draws its own loop and `freshStart` (:2105-2119) wipes the real selection.
- **Scroll cannot be stopped in pointer code.** The pad has `touch-action: pan-x pan-y pinch-zoom` (:832); iPadOS reads it at touchstart, so a palm that rests before the Pencil lands is not covered. LS-57 treats scroll as best effort.
- **The margin loop has no net at all** (src/paper/PaperFrame.tsx:1778-1795): no input-machine entry, no 4 s limit, no hover check; a lost lift leaves the line until the next Pencil-down.
- **In-page loops:** `lassoMove` (:2212) never calls `hoverEnds` (:849); hover moves refresh the 4 s limit (src/app/inputMachine.ts:123, :238).
- **Two routes to "the next loop acts on the old selection":** (1) a stroke that starts inside the old dashed box, widened by 8 px, moves the old selection by design (:2186-2193), so a loop begun there never starts; (2) `staleGesture` (:2089-2098) treats a new Pencil-down within 250 ms of hover moves as the same press and ignores it, so an old move goes on (I: the best candidate for the owner's fault, not proven).
- **Sizes:** A1 is about 5 h, not 3. Split: A1a the nets in the controller (LS-50 to LS-56), A1b the margin loop (LS-54), A1c the palm (LS-57, LS-58). Seam: a new file (for example src/ink/lassoNets.ts) with pure functions; small tagged hooks in `lassoDown`, `lassoMove`, `lassoUp` only.

## 2. Text selection (builder)

- **Picking the Lasso takes the focus away.** `watchKeyboardPolicy` (src/editor/keyboardPolicy.ts:231-237) and `flushModes` (src/editor/editPolicy.ts:93-100) blur the box; the editor still remembers the cursor's place. The first loop then wipes it (`freshStart`). So "the box that holds the cursor" must be "the box typed in last", remembered at the tool switch, and the text-level gate must run **before** `freshStart` (one small hook in `lassoDown`).
- **Trap:** `scopeOf` (src/paper/v2/oneBarOps.ts:28-31) returns "box" when the editor has no focus. New code must never use `hasFocus()` to mean "there is a cursor".
- **The "half a letter" rule is not what today's circle code does:** `glyphsNear` drops each letter's width and height (src/editor/circleSelect.ts:164-170), so `glyphInLoop` (:125) uses the centre only. The text level needs its own geometry; circleSelect.ts (Text tool) is not touched.
- **A highlight exists already** (`ln-sel` decorations in src/editor/ownSelection.ts; `selectTextRange` in src/editor/lassoWords.ts:174-186). The handles are the large new piece.
- **Scribble over selected words needs focus**, which the Lasso takes away. So under the Lasso a scratch-out over the selection is our own gesture (the ink engine already reads zigzags); writing over a selected word (T26) belongs to the Text tool.
- **Sizes:** A4 about 6 h (split: highlight 2 h, handles 4 h); A5 4 h; A6, A7 3 h; A8 2 h; A9 5 h (split: scratch-out under the Lasso; T26 in the Text tool). Seam: new folder `src/editor/textLevel/`.

## 3. The smart bar (builder)

- **The box bar is tied to boxes** (`OneBar.tsx` takes a box and its editor; `PinnedBoxBar` finds its target from box attributes). Reusable: the shell (grip, dock model, place store, pops). Needed: a selection model (cursor, words, box, drawing, picture, mix), an actions layer per kind that calls the ink engine's existing functions, one mount at page level, new buttons and texts.
- **Six pieces in the code, not three:** MiniToolbar (744 lines), SelectionBarV8 (455), LassoMenuSheet, MixedMenu (InkSurface.tsx:866), the old box bar, OneBar with PinnedBoxBar.
- **The size limit is real:** 313,095 of 320,000 bytes on 20 Nov (about 6.9 KB left); the bar must load lazily.
- **Two bars at once exist today** in some Pencil flows (`afterSelect`, PageEditor.tsx:857-865; `selectTextRange`), which SB-N6 forbids.
- The new switches need entries and texts in src/app/flags.ts (hot file).
- **Sizes:** B3 8 h (split: model and mount; text states), B4 3 h, B5 8 h (split), B6 4 h, B7 7 h (split: storage and order; Settings screen), B8 3 h, B9 4 h.

## 4. What the test tools can and cannot do (tester, V)

- `e2e/realPen.ts` is a mouse relabelled as a pen: no touch and pen together, no pointercancel, no two pointers.
- `e2e/circleTrace.ts` replays a trace with made-up pressure and buttons and drops capture and focus events. **Part A2 must upgrade the replay** to send what the trace holds, or a real trace cannot become a faithful test.
- Replayed events are not trusted by the browser: Scribble and Apple's bar are never exercised by any replay.
- The 4 second tests wait for real time (`waitForTimeout(4500)`). New tests use Playwright's fake clock.
- The iPad-size project runs only a list of files (playwright.config.ts): every new test file must be added there, or it never runs at iPad size.
- Running the Lasso tests with the new switch off and on doubles their run time.

## 5. Numbers for vague words (tester)

Half a letter = 50 % (test 49 and 51). Taps add up within 1000 ms (test 999 and 1001) and within a stated distance. The palm moment = 700 ms until the trace says otherwise (test 699 and 701). "Never waits" = the new loop's first point is taken in the same frame. "Gone within 150 ms" = the outline is absent by the frame after the fade. "Keeps its place" = the bar's left, top and width within 1 px. The purple = one colour value with a stated contrast.

## 6. Cases the specs did not answer (both lenses)

Lasso: a palm that rests before the Pencil lands; a palm still down while the Pencil is in the air; pointercancel in the middle of a loop; the app hidden or the iPad turned in the middle of a loop; Undo while an outline shows.
Text: a circle half in and half out of the box; over two lines; over just a space; over words and a drawing together; a handle dragged past the other; a box with a link or a widget in it; Undo after Delete; scrolling while words are selected.
Bar: the selection changes while a strip is open; nothing is selected and the bar is pinned; Auto off (which bar for a mix); a saved order that names a button that no longer exists; Paste refused by the iPad.

## 7. What a green computer test does not prove (tester)

LS-50 to LS-52, LS-57, LS-58, LS-N1, LS-N2, LS-N4, LS-N5 (the cause is a guess until the owner's trace; the fault shows 1 or 2 times in 5 to 10 uses, so 20 of 20 on a computer says nothing about it); "Scribble stays on"; TS-N1, TS-N2, TS-N5; SB-N5, SB-N7; a held button's name with the Pencil; docking by Pencil drag; every time value on the real device; "unchanged" holds only for the scenarios that exist. Each of these is decided by the owner's iPad check.

## 8. The quiet recorder (Part A2), from the tester

Record per event: order number, both time stamps, type, pointer id and type, primary or not, button, buttons, pressure, tilt, width and height, position, target, trusted or not, cancelled or not; touch events with their type; capture gained and lost; hover (over, out, enter, leave); click; focus, blur, page hidden, selection change, text input; and our own state: tool, Lasso gesture, input machine, selection, number of outlines, editable or not, the 4 second timer, and the reason a gesture ended.
Never: draw on the page, cancel or stop an event, capture a pointer, use the page's timers, or do heavy work per event. Passive listeners on the window, an array in memory, the copy panel only after the stroke and outside the page. Off by default.
