**Status: DRAFT. Not approved. Opus builds nothing from this page.** A line is approved only by the owner's yes. Section 4 is not final: several of its lines wait for proofs by Opus in the real app (section 8), and they are confirmed with the owner after that. When the page is approved, Opus moves it to `docs/features/`.

Where it comes from: [the talk notes](phase-1b-notebook-talk.md) (the owner's words, the code check, the walk-through, the audits). The plan it belongs to: [the plan after phase 1A](../plan-after-1a.md).

## How to read this page

The owner, 25 Nov: "It should be a goal, not a particular condition." "We don't need to limit the quality of output." And on numbers: Fable may set them, "just make sure that it's not too limiting."

- **Goal.** What good looks like. Claude and Opus are free in how they reach it. It is proved by a look: Opus looks at the real result, then the owner looks at it on the real device, against the page the owner approved (section 7).
- **Fence.** What must hold every time, or must never happen. Each fence has a test with a result a program can see. The few numbers in the fences are the owner's own, from earlier decisions.
- **When.** What the app does when the owner does something. Each one has a test through the real screen.
- **Situations.** Where those tests must hold (section 5).

An id never changes. "Exists" means the code check found it in the app; "new" means it did not; "to prove" means nobody has proved it yet. "iPad" means only the real iPad can prove it. "Fable's" means the line is not the owner's own words.

## 1. The objective, in the owner's words (25 Nov 2026)

- "I open my app, a notebook should be there with multiple chapters and subpages."
- "It should have a summary kind of note section ... Also a detailed section will be there, easily accessible."
- "A mind map will be there of everything ... which I can just see in a glimpse and get a complete idea of everything."
- "A review card will be there based on this."
- "As much accuracy and depth possible."
- "It should be very visually appealing." "This way our notes will be very interactive." "Make it more like human." And: "Too much information is harmful."
- "It should be easily readable on a tablet. And it should be easily customizable on the PC. That is stage 1 goal."
- "The notebook should not have any glitches. It should be very smooth experience like Apple."
- "Just make sure that any feature you add should be easy to customize."

**The first real notebook:** Claude skills. What they are, how they work, if they work, the best practices. "I read it, after a few days I forgot it."

**"I can use the app every day"** (Fable's sentence; the owner agreed to it on 25 Nov): on the PC, Claude writes a notebook, the app imports it in full, and the owner reads it and customizes it. On the tablet the owner reads. On the phone the owner answers recall cards.

**What this spec replaces, by the owner's newer word of 25 Nov:**

- 22 Nov, "Review and recall cards are out of stage 1": replaced for the cards that a notebook brings (NB-10).
- 22 Nov, dashboards in stage 2: replaced for the front page of a notebook (NB-50, NB-51).
- 22 Nov, the open clash on mind maps, databases, pictures and the paper page (stage 1 or 2): they are in stage 1 as far as a notebook that Claude writes needs them.
- 22 Nov, "Pencil fine work stays in stage 2": it stays. The shapes that hold text (section 4.4) are box and shape work, decided for stage 1 on 25 Nov.

## 7. The approved look (not there yet)

The way of working asks for a picture that the owner approved before a look is built. There is none: the owner rejected the drawn samples of 25 Nov, and the page made in the real app was plain because tools failed. So:

- The parts that make the tools work come first (section 4.4).
- Then one chapter is made in the real app, in its frame, and shown to the owner on the tablet and on the PC, in Night and in Day.
- The owner's yes to that page makes it the approved look. Until then no goal of section 3 about the look counts as reached.

Each part of this spec also fills the "Design and quality check" that every spec of this project carries (owner, 22 Nov), and it sits behind an on-off switch on the preview, with the one-tap test preset, as the owner's delivery rules say.

## 9. Direction for what is built after stage 1 (gets its lines when its turn comes)

- **The map of a whole notebook** as one more view of its chapters.
- **The row of options as a switch for the database:** a tap on Mind map, Summary or Notes shows the database in that form, with the filters and tools that belong to it; columns for the mind map, the review cards and the summary to filter by (owner, 25 Nov).
