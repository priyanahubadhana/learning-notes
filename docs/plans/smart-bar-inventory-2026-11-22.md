## 3. Doubts for the owner (A or B, my pick, the case against)

- **D1. What is "Edit"?** A: the Edit Text button (box and mix; puts the cursor at the end of the words; OD:527, SB:88), drawn with its name and its own icon. B: an "Edit / Study" preset switch visible on the bar itself (REF shows it only in Settings). **Pick A**, and the drawing state gets "Convert to text" under ⋯ as today. Against: if you meant B, the bar still never shows which preset is on.
- **D2. When does the pin show?** A: always, right after the grip, on every bar. B: only after the bar has been dragged (the approved box bar, BB:252). **Pick A** (you expect to see it). Against: one more button; on a phone it pushes one button into ⋯.
- **D3. One fixed tail.** A: every selected state (words, box, drawing, picture, mix, both presets, phone) ends with Cut, Copy, Delete, ⋯▾ in that order (SB:34, OD:548). B: Study keeps its drawn set (Copy only, Delete in ⋯; OD:519, OD:537) and a box keeps Copy in ⋯ (OD:434). **Pick A.** Against: the Study bar gets two buttons longer and the approved box bar changes.
- **D4. Paste ▾ everywhere.** A: Paste ▾ sits just before the tail in every state, mixed included (OD:495, OD:505 nothing dropped). B: Paste ▾ on the bar only for caret, words and box; under ⋯ elsewhere. **Pick A.** Against: pasting over a drawing or a mix is rare and adds width.
- **D5. Mixed selection.** A: Edit Text, Resize, Cut, Copy, Duplicate, Paste ▾, Delete on the bar (OD:537); Colour, Thickness, Make card, Add to summary under ⋯. B: Colour and Thickness also on the bar, as for a drawing. **Pick A.** Against: changing ink colour in a mix takes two taps. (Mixed is stage 2, SM:53.)
- **D6. Hold menu and Action bar together.** A: a hold (iPad) or right-click (PC) opens the Hold menu (Cut, Copy, Paste ▾, Delete, ⋯; PM:7) at the finger or pointer, and the Action bar hides while it is open (one bar at a time, SB-N6). B: a hold or right-click on a selection opens the Action bar's ⋯ at that spot, no separate menu. **Pick A** (approved strip, familiar on PC). Against: two lists for the same selection to keep in step.
- **D7. The ✕.** A: no ✕ on a following bar (it goes when the selection ends: click away or Esc; Deselect in ⋯); a pinned or docked bar gets a minimise ✕ at its end (UP:9, shell-spec.md:88), because it stays with nothing selected. B: a ✕ at the end of every bar. **Pick A.** Against: the bar looks slightly different when pinned.
- **D8. App frame against a following bar.** PR:15 lists "the action bar" as part of the fixed frame; OD:546 and SB:36 make it follow the selection. A: it follows the selection; once pinned or docked it becomes part of the frame and nothing covers it. B: on PC and tablet it sits fixed in the frame under the Tool bar, changing its buttons in place. **Pick A** (21 Nov place order, approved pictures 15-19). Against: PR:15 is the newer text (22 Nov). Also asked here: do Study and Auto change the box, drawing and mix sets, or only words (silent; the customiser has a set per kind, OD:548)? Pick: one set per kind per preset.

## 5. Rules the redraw must obey

- **App frame (PR:15):** Tool bar, Action bar, side panel solid and fixed; pop-ups, pickers and toasts live inside the page area, never across the frame (see D8).
- **Q4 (PR:85):** menus radius 8; inputs 8; cards, pickers, panels 20; sheets 24; dialogs 24; toast 14; chips pill.
- **Q5 (PR:88):** toast = pill, bottom-right on every device, 2.6 s, 6 s with Undo; small ✕ at its top-left, Undo on the other side.
- **Q11 (PR:106):** Tool bar 64 px high.
- **Q15 (PR:118):** names Action bar, Tool bar, pickers, Hold menu; never "smart bar", "selection bar", "strip".
- **Q17 (PR:124):** every target 44 px always; Compact tightens spacing only.
- **Q18 (PR:127):** one ⋯, last, with a small ▾ when more options exist; it ends with Customise bar…; no separate More control.
- Also kept: Delete the only red button (OD:527); names on hover, never on a finger hold (OD:549); icon stroke 1.75 (PR:76); the bar never covers the selection or leaves the screen (SB:49).
