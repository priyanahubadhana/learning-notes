## Read this first

The owner set three stages on 22 Nov. **Stage 1, the working app, for the owner:** what the owner needs to use the app every day. **Stage 2, the premium app, for the owner:** what makes it feel premium and complete for one person. **Stage 3, for everyone:** what only other users, or many users, need. The rule for sorting: "If I were using the app alone tomorrow, would I miss it? If yes, stage 1. Would I enjoy it but not miss it? Stage 2. Does only someone else need it? Stage 3."

From now on every spec and every part card carries its **Stage** in its header, and the site shows a stage badge and a list per stage. Something can move between stages only through this page and the owner's yes.

**Goal of stage 1 (proposed):** "I use Learning Notes every day on the PC for my own notes, and read them on the iPad, without a blocking bug for a week." Stage 1 is two phases: PC-1 (this one) and the Pencil basics phase after it.

- The owner's Q5 and Q6 answers change to "later, by stage", recorded as the owner's decision of 22 Nov.
- Estimate: about 11 to 14 working days from the start of stage 0 (was 17 to 20).
