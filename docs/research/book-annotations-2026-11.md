# Book annotations into notes — research (2026-11-10, item 43)

Question: how do reading apps bring highlights, typed notes and handwriting from a book/PDF into notes? Our bug: BookInk strokes and highlights show up as loose ink under "Cards" on the book's note page, with no source.

## Lessons

1. All the study apps turn each annotation into **one item with a source link**; nobody copies raw ink onto a notes canvas.
2. Ink is noisy: Zotero refuses to extract it by default. Best practice: a **snapshot image of the area**.
3. Group by chapter + filter by colour (Kindle, Apple Books, Zotero) is standard.
4. Highlight to flashcard in one tap (MarginNote, Readwise) fits our Cards section.
