# Box grid research (10 Nov 2026)

Question: owner decision on bug-batch item 26: boxes never overlap; they snap into a tidy grid (1-3 per row, at most 6 per area), like Windows Snap Layouts and Notion columns. Plan: [plans/box-grid-plan.md](../plans/box-grid-plan.md).

## Findings

1. **Windows 11 Snap Layouts / Snap Groups.** If you drag a window to the top of the screen, a small strip of layouts appears (halves, thirds, quadrants). Dropping on a zone snaps the window there, then Snap Assist offers to fill the other zones. Touch works too. The arrangement is saved as one "Snap Group". Takeaway: **show the zones only while dragging, preview the result before release, treat the row as one unit.** Sources: [Thurrott feature focus](https://www.thurrott.com/windows/windows-11/252475/windows-11-feature-focus-snap-layouts), [Windows Central 22H2](https://www.windowscentral.com/whats-new-snap-assist-windows-11-version-22h2), [Microsoft Q&A](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/answers/questions/2337358/how-to-use-snap-layouts-and-snap-groups-in-windows), [Pureinfotech](https://pureinfotech.com/snap-keyboard-mouse-windows-11/).
2. **Notion columns.** Drag a block by its handle to another block's left or right edge and a blue vertical guide appears. Dropping makes columns. You resize by dragging the gap between columns, and the widths stay proportional. There is no hard cap, but more than about 5 is advised against. On phones, columns stack (the right column goes under the left). Takeaway: **an edge-of-box drop target, a thin accent guide, a draggable divider, widths as fractions, stacking on phones.** Sources: [Thomas Frank, rows and columns](https://thomasjfrank.com/learn-notion/page-layout-rows-and-columns/), [multi-column layouts](https://thomasjfrank.com/how-to-create-multi-column-layouts-in-notion/), [McGill guide](https://classroom-physicists.physics.mcgill.ca/documentation/notes/quick-guide-to-using-notion/columns-headings-dividers), [Landmark Labs](https://www.landmarklabs.co/insights/notion-columns).
3. **Figma auto layout.** There are separate flows: horizontal (which can wrap) and grid (fixed rows and columns, cells can span). Takeaway: **a fixed grid (no wrapping) is simpler and predictable.** Source: [Figma help: flows](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/31289464393751).
4. **OneNote / GoodNotes / Freeform / Craft** (product knowledge, not re-checked this session). OneNote containers are free-position and overlap, which is the messy page the owner wants to avoid. GoodNotes text boxes are free, overlap and have z-order. Freeform has alignment guides and snapping on a free canvas. Craft lays out blocks in flow with side-by-side cards. None of them gives a strict grid, so our design takes its look from Notion and its drag preview from Windows.
5. **CSS.** Each row is a `display:grid` with `grid-template-columns` in `fr` from the stored fractions and a gap token. On phones it becomes 1 column. The row height is the tallest column (auto). Standard and cheap, with no layout math in JS.

## Conclusion

Rows of 1-3 columns with fractional widths and 1-2 boxes stacked per column (a 2x3 maximum, so 6 per area). Dragging shows Notion-style edge targets plus a live Windows-style preview. Phones stack.
