## 0. Findings that change the plan (read this first)

1. **Why the Pen writes text instead of ink (R4-20).** WebKit finds Scribble targets with `Page::editableElementsInRect`, a rect hit test with the `CollectMultipleElements` option that collects every editable root under the stroke, not only the top element [verified: [WebKit Page.cpp](https://github.com/WebKit/WebKit/blob/main/Source/WebCore/page/Page.cpp)]. A transparent ink pad on top does not hide the editable text below it. Our editor stays `contenteditable` while the Pen is active [verified: our code, `editor/PageEditor.tsx` `editable={!frozen}`; `ink/controller.ts` only toggles the pad]. **Fix:** while an ink tool is active the page is not editable (`editor.isEditable = false`), the page content is `inert`, and focus is dropped. Editability is decided by `hasEditableStyle()`, and WebKit treats `inert` like `pointer-events: none` in hit testing [verified: [WebKit bug 229330](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=229330), [bug 230681](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=230681)]. The same function also adds the focused element if it intersects [verified: Page.cpp], so blurring matters.
2. **Why Pencil taps on the bar write a comma (R4-17).** The top bar is `sticky` inside the scrolling `<main>` [verified: our code, `App.tsx` `<main className="min-h-0 flex-1 overflow-auto">`, `editor/BarStack.tsx`]. When the page is scrolled, editable text lies _under_ the bar, so the multi-element hit test above finds it. **Fix:** bars live outside the scroller (no editable content is ever under chrome), plus a stylus `touchstart` cancel on chrome with our own pen-tap activation (the pattern AFFiNE used: "Pen taps on menu and radio controls activate on pointerup" [verified: [AFFiNE PR 15525](https://github.com/toeverything/AFFiNE/pull/15525)]).
3. **Lost Pencil strokes and "taps not registering".** Excalidraw had the same symptom (strokes skipped when written fast) and fixed it by calling `preventDefault()` on `touchstart` for the drawing surface [verified: [excalidraw PR 4705](https://github.com/excalidraw/excalidraw/pull/4705), [issue 3955](https://github.com/excalidraw/excalidraw/issues/3955)]. WebKit bug 217430 (missing pointer events with Scribble) was marked fixed in Feb 2022 [verified: [bug 217430](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=217430)], but Excalidraw still needed the cancel. We cancel stylus `touchstart` already; add `touchmove` too [verified idea: [Apple forums 662874](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/662874)].
4. **Undo opens the keyboard (R4-16).** `runHistory` calls `view.focus()` after every Undo/Redo [verified: our code, `editor/historyActions.ts`]. Remove it.
5. **Our selection bar can push the iOS callout away.** For a selection in a contenteditable, WebKit gathers "evasion rects" from clickable, non-editable elements just _above_ the selection and asks the callout bar to avoid them [verified: [WebKit r241971](https://trac.webkit.org/r241971)]. So: our bar directly above the selection; the native bar moves elsewhere. The callout cannot be removed from a focused editable [verified: [ProseMirror forum](https://discuss.prosemirror.net/t/how-to-disable-the-mobile-safari-selection-menu/2581)]. A selection we draw ourselves on a non-editable, unfocused editor has no native callout at all [unverified, reasoning: no DOM selection, nothing for iOS to show].
6. **Keyboard and page jumps (R4-15).** `interactive-widget` is still not supported in Safari up to 26.x [verified: [caniuse](https://caniuse.com/mdn-html_elements_meta_name_viewport_interactive-widget_resizes-content), [bram.us Sep 2026](https://www.bram.us/2026/09/11/webkit-supports-interactive-widget-and-hopefully-safari-will-too/)]. iOS 26 has open bugs where `visualViewport.offsetTop` and height do not reset after the keyboard closes [verified: [Apple forums 800125](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/800125), [WebKit bug 300523](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=300523)]. Developers report the most robust layout is a normal flex column (bars in normal flow) rather than JS that follows the viewport [verified as reports: [yomotherboard Q&A](https://yomotherboard.com/?p=499353)]. Our top bar currently _follows_ the keyboard pan [verified: `BarStack.tsx` line 267 comment "The keyboard can pan the screen under a sticky bar: follow it"]. **Fix:** flex-column shell, top bar never follows the viewport, our own caret reveal via ProseMirror `handleScrollToSelection`.
7. **Caret from Pencil coordinates.** `document.caretPositionFromPoint` exists in Safari 26.2+ [verified: [web-features](https://web-platform-dx.github.io/web-features-explorer/features/document-caretpositionfrompoint.json)]. `getCoalescedEvents` and `getPredictedEvents` arrived in iOS Safari 18.2, and coalesced events there lacked `pointerId` and `target` [verified: [caniuse](https://caniuse.com/mdn-api_pointerevent_getpredictedevents), [Flutter engine commit](https://dart.googlesource.com/external/github.com/flutter/engine/+/23447c4674b0837ada66d29099147e05a695cec4)]. Never read `pointerId` from a coalesced event.
8. **The blank grey area (R4-9c) may be canvas memory.** iOS Safari draws canvases transparent once total canvas memory passes about 384 MB, and refuses canvases above 16,777,216 px [verified: [pqina total memory](https://www.pqina.nl/blog/total-canvas-memory-use-exceeds-the-maximum-limit/), [pqina area](https://pqina.nl/blog/canvas-area-exceeds-the-maximum-limit/), [react-pdf #1020](https://github.com/wojtekmaj/react-pdf/issues/1020)]. Our ink keeps three canvases plus one cached bitmap per visible layer, each a little taller than the screen at full pixel ratio [verified: `docs/features/ink.md` "Speed"]. Hypothesis, not proven: a canvas budget fixes it. pdf.js will need the same budget.
9. **Piper voices are GPL now.** The maintained Piper moved to `OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl` under GPL-3.0; the old MIT repo was archived [verified: [piper1-gpl releases](https://github.com/OHF-voice/piper1-gpl/releases)]. Earlier research said MIT: corrected. Kokoro via `kokoro-js` is Apache-2.0 [verified: npm registry `kokoro-js` 1.2.1 Apache-2.0]. Its model is roughly 80 MB [unverified], larger than Cloudflare Pages' 25 MiB per-file limit [verified: [Cloudflare Pages limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/pages/platform/limits)], so it cannot be self-hosted on our Pages site as one file.
10. **BlockNote already has Table, Toggle list, File, Audio and Video blocks.** Our schema leaves them out [verified: `node_modules/@blocknote/core` 0.56.0 `defaultBlocks.d.ts` and `src/editor/schema.tsx`]. "Table: coming" is a schema and mapping job, not a new editor.
11. **Ink anchored to paragraphs is the right call.** OneNote does not lock ink and text together, so reflow makes ink drift; users call it a design flaw [verified as community answers: [Microsoft Q&A](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/answers/questions/5778657/facing-an-issue-in-microsoft-onenote-between-ipad)]. Apple Notes offers "Insert Space Above" on drawings [verified: [Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/en-ca/guide/ipad/aside/ipada87a6078/ipados)]. The owner's choice (ink moves with its paragraph, already built) avoids OneNote's complaint.

## (a) iPad Safari + Apple Pencil on the web

### Scribble

- Scribble works on standard editable web content, including any `contenteditable` element [verified: [WWDC20 Meet Scribble](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2020/10106), [firt.dev iOS 14](https://firt.dev/ios-14/)].
- Writing near (about 50 px around) an editable element also counts [unverified: [firt.dev](https://firt.dev/ios-14/), 2020].
- Target lookup = `editableElementsInRect`: hit test of a rect with `ReadOnly, Active, CollectMultipleElements, DisallowUserAgentShadowContent, AllowVisibleChildFrameContentOnly`; keeps a node if it is an editable text control or has editable style (its root editable element); also adds the focused element when it intersects [verified: [Page.cpp](https://github.com/WebKit/WebKit/blob/main/Source/WebCore/page/Page.cpp)].
- Hit testing skips `pointer-events: none`, and since 2021 also `inert` subtrees [verified: [bug 229330](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=229330), [bug 230681](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=230681)].
- No web API turns Scribble off per element; native apps have `UIScribbleInteraction` [verified: [WWDC20](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2020/10106)]. Users can turn Scribble off only system-wide [verified: [DEV: Scribble interfering with canvas](https://dev.to/skyjinxx/ipados-scribble-interfering-with-apple-pencil-canvas-drawing-in-web-browsers-3ba7)].
- AFFiNE (a web editor in a native shell) needed a native bridge to classify Pencil vs finger and to steer Scribble; its review flagged that committing strokes on `pointercancel` can turn a cancelled gesture into a real drop [verified: [PR 15525](https://github.com/toeverything/AFFiNE/pull/15525)]. We have no native shell, so we use editability and layout.
- **Recipe adopted** (all parts reasoned from the verified facts above; the combination is [unverified] until the Pencil lab on the owner's iPad):
  1. Ink tool active → `editor.isEditable = false`, `inert` on the page content wrapper (title, editor, chips, database blocks), blur, title `readOnly`. The ink overlay must sit _outside_ the inert wrapper.
  2. Lasso active → page not editable (no `inert`, so `elementFromPoint`/`posAtCoords` still work).
  3. Text active → editable; Scribble allowed (owner test T1 passed).
  4. No editable content under any bar: bars outside the scroller.
  5. Chrome (bars, pills, cards, menus): stylus `touchstart` → `preventDefault()`, action on `pointerup`, ignore the late native `click`.

### Pointer events, palm and latency

- `pointerType` is `"pen"` for the Pencil; Safari touches carry `touchType: "stylus"` [verified: [scrapbox](https://scrapbox.io/nishio-en/Distinguishing_between_direct_touch_and_Pencil_touch_on_the_iPad)].
- Once the Pencil touches, iPadOS may stop sending finger events; tldraw had to work around it [verified: [tldraw blog](https://tldraw.dev/blog/a-touchy-subject)]; palm touches can be cancelled by the OS [verified: [Apple forums 847321](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/847321)]. No palm touch type on the web [verified: AFFiNE PR description].
- `getCoalescedEvents` / `getPredictedEvents`: iOS 18.2+; coalesced events lacked `pointerId`/`target` in 18.2 [verified, see §0.7].
- `desynchronized` canvas hint: not documented for Safari [unverified]; keep it as a harmless hint.
- Pencil hover: hover works in Safari for UI on M2+ iPads [verified: [Apple support community](https://origin-discussions2-us-dr-prz.apple.com/thread/254330124)]; whether pages get `pointermove` with `pointerType: "pen"` while hovering is [unverified] → log it in the Pencil lab, never depend on it.
- Pencil Pro squeeze, barrel roll and double-tap are not exposed to web pages [unverified; no web API found].
- Excalidraw's pen mode keeps two-finger pinch and pan working while the pen draws [verified: [PR 4725](https://github.com/excalidraw/excalidraw/pull/4725)].

### Caret placement and selection

- ProseMirror `posAtCoords` returns null outside the editor and is ambiguous at line wraps; `coordsAtPos` gives the rect back [verified: [ProseMirror reference](https://prosemirror.net/docs/ref/), [prosemirror-view changelog](https://github.com/ProseMirror/prosemirror-view/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md)].
- `handleScrollToSelection` lets us take over "scroll the selection into view"; `scrollMargin` / `scrollThreshold` tune it [verified: [ProseMirror reference](https://prosemirror.net/docs/ref/)]. BlockNote 0.56 passes `_tiptapOptions` (incl. `editorProps`) to Tiptap and has an `isEditable` setter [verified: `node_modules/@blocknote/core/types/src/editor/BlockNoteEditor.d.ts` lines 258, 606–614]; `_tiptapOptions` is internal, so wrap it in one file.
- `-webkit-touch-callout: none` does not stop the selection callout in editable content [verified: [ProseMirror forum](https://discuss.prosemirror.net/t/how-to-disable-the-mobile-safari-selection-menu/2581), [MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/CSS/-webkit-touch-callout)].
- Custom clipboard formats do not survive iOS paste [verified: [WebKit bug 184271](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=184271)].

### Keyboard, viewport, page jumps

- See §0.6. Safari resizes the visual viewport only; fixed bars get covered or offset [verified: [Modern Web Weekly 61](https://modernwebweekly.substack.com/p/modern-web-weekly-61?open=false), [Apple forums 824765](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/824765)].
- In standalone PWAs the viewport can stay shrunk after the first keyboard [verified as a report: [DEV](https://dev.to/cederhook/fixing-the-ios-standalone-pwa-keyboard-bug-that-shrinks-your-viewport-for-good-63d)].
- **Approach adopted:** `height: 100dvh` flex column; bars in normal flow outside the page scroller; `focus({ preventScroll: true })` everywhere; no native `scrollIntoView`; caret reveal by our own delta scroll on the page scroller; Pencil taps never open the keyboard (already built). [unverified until owner check]

### Hit targets

- Apple HIG 44×44 pt; WCAG 2.5.8 AA 24×24 CSS px with spacing, 2.5.5 AAA 44×44 [verified via secondary: [Deque](https://dequeuniversity.com/rules/attest-ios/1.0/touch-target-size), [Adrian Roselli](https://adrianroselli.com/2019/06/target-size-and-2-5-5.html)].
