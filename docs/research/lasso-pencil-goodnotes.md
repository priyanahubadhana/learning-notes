# Lasso and Pencil taps: how GoodNotes and Notability do it, and what we change

Date: 2026-11-07. Method: web searches and page reads (GoodNotes' support site refuses automated reads, so its lasso rules come from search snippets of the support articles and the GoodNotes feedback forum), plus a read of our own pointer pipeline. Marks: **[verified + link]** = a source says it; **[unverified]** = secondary, a user report, or my inference. Nothing here was tried on a real iPad; the owner checks at the top of [owner-checks-pending.md](../plans/owner-checks-pending.md) do that.

## Part A. What the owner saw, and the cause in our code

Five reports, one family of causes: under the Lasso tool the page text still receives the Pencil, and the parts of the ink UI that sit over the text (handles, ruler body) do not cancel the stylus touch, so iPadOS reads the gesture as Scribble or as a scroll.

| Report                                                    | Root cause (our code)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1. The lasso "reaches through" to the text                | The Lasso only watched the page (`lassoDown` on the editor container, the pad off). `penSelect.ts` runs first (document capture) and starts a text drag-selection on every Pencil down over words; its `select(anchor, pos)` grew a text highlight while the loop was drawn. `keyboardPolicy.ts` also marked the block (outline flash).                                                                                                      |
| 2. The lasso fails over an empty block                    | On words, `penSelect` cancelled the stylus `touchstart` (so iPadOS left the touch to us). On an empty block or blank paper nothing cancelled it, so iPadOS took the touch (scroll or Scribble), the page got `pointercancel` and the lasso was dropped.                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 3. Handles select the text instead of acting              | The handle buttons sit over editable text and never cancelled the stylus touch. WebKit's Scribble hit test collects every editable element under the stroke rectangle, overlay or not [verified: [WebKit Page.cpp](https://github.com/WebKit/WebKit/blob/main/Source/WebCore/page/Page.cpp), see deep-research-2026-11.md §1], so the Pencil gesture became a Scribble selection gesture on the words and our transform got `pointercancel`. |
| 4. Micro-strokes on taps                                  | Every pointer down/up under the Pen made a stroke, even a 1-point, 20 ms tap. No tap filter existed; nothing treated two quick taps as a gesture.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 5. Double/triple tap selects poorly                       | Three modules counted or reacted to taps: `penSelect` (own double-tap word rule and a caret-placing `select(pos, pos)` on every down), `tapSelect` (count on pointer up, then a 0 ms timer) and `keyboardPolicy` (two-tap rule). The second tap of a double tap went down a different path than the first (the block was now "active"), and the third tap collapsed the word selection before the line selection landed. Under the Pen, taps never reached `tapSelect` (the pad is not inside the editor). |
| Ruler drag selects the text (coordinator, 7 Nov)          | The ruler body was `pointer-events: none` by design ("lets everything through"); only the three handles took touches. A drag on the body was a native text drag.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Magnifier unclear                                         | No hint, a thin dashed box and a small ⠿ handle; nothing says "write here, it lands there".                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

## Part B. GoodNotes and Notability

### Lasso (GoodNotes 6)

- Selecting: drag a rectangle, or draw a free-hand loop; objects are selected when the loop encloses **or simply touches** them [verified from support snippets: [GoodNotes Lasso](https://support.goodnotes.com/hc/en-us/articles/7353695644175)]. Tapping the selection opens the menu: Take screenshot, Resize, Colour, Convert, Cut/Copy, Delete [verified: same].
- "Included in Selection" filter: Handwriting, Images, Text boxes (or All). Example from support: to pick handwriting lying on an image, turn Images off [verified: snippets of [the same article](https://support.goodnotes.com/hc/en-us/articles/7353695644175)]. Typed text in GoodNotes lives in text boxes, which the lasso treats as **whole objects**: a loop never selects a range of characters, and a text box is moved whole even if only part was circled (a user complaint) [verified: [GoodNotes feedback](https://feedback.goodnotes.com/forums/191274-customer-suggestions-for-goodnotes-apple/suggestions/50597972--lasso-stop-making-lasso-the-default?page=6)].
- Handles: white squares scale, blue circles resize, a white protruding circle rotates; "select outside the selection to apply the change" [verified: snippets of [GoodNotes support](https://support.goodnotes.com/hc/en-us/articles/10779441732111-Edit-and-reflow-handwriting-with-the-Lasso-tool)]. A tap outside deselects [unverified: [feedback thread](https://feedback.goodnotes.com/forums/191274-customer-suggestions-for-goodnotes/suggestions/40448485-improve-lasso-tool-improvements)].
- The Pencil never scrolls the page in GoodNotes; a finger does [unverified: common knowledge of the app, not found on a support page].

### Notability

- The Select tool draws around handwriting; put a finger or the stylus inside the outline and drag to move [verified: [Notability Select Tool](https://support.gingerlabs.com/hc/en-us/articles/360018646412-Select-Tool)]. Two fingers inside the outline twist to rotate and pinch to scale [unverified: a student tutorial]. Tap an image to show its borders [unverified: a user comment].

### Selection gesture research (Ink & Switch)

- Clockwise loop selects only fully enclosed ink; counter-clockwise also takes crossed ink; a scrub control moves between "connected ink that overlaps", "strokes that overlap", "exactly inside", "fully inside" [verified: [Ink & Switch, selection gestures](https://inkandswitch.com/ink/notes/selection-gestures)]. Too clever for us now; our rule stays "half the points inside".

### Taps versus ink

- No app documents its numbers. Patterns from patents: a tap is judged by **size** (too big an allowance turns commas and apostrophes into taps; tiny handwriting the other way) [verified: [US 5,864,635](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/5864635)]; movement during a tap is mostly **slip**, so coordinates are held at the first point until a threshold is crossed [verified: [Wacom US 10,452,197](https://patents.google.com/patent/US10452197)]; a stylus held past a **time** threshold is a different action [verified: [US 7,081,889](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/7081889)]; tap detection is down-and-up within a time window regardless of force [verified: [Apple US 12,681,635](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/12681635)]. A PencilKit developer cleared drawings under about 10 pt in both directions as taps [verified: [Apple forums](https://origin-devforums.apple.com/forums/profile/OneHourIndieDev)]. Pencil Pro users report blobs at stroke starts because the pen registers about 1 mm before touching [unverified: [Reddit](https://redlib.hbubli.cc/r/GoodNotes/comments/1h0mkf3/is_my_ipad_apple_pencil_defective)].
- Scribble on iPad Safari swallows or cancels short, quick Pencil strokes that look like gestures; the only sure cure a developer found was turning Scribble off [verified: [DEV: Scribble interfering with canvas drawing](https://dev.to/skyjinxx/ipados-scribble-interfering-with-apple-pencil-canvas-drawing-in-web-browsers-3ba7)]. The Pencil's hardware double-tap and squeeze are not exposed to web pages [unverified, no API found].

### Ruler and Zoom window

- Apple's Markup ruler: drag with one finger to move, touch and hold with two fingers and rotate to turn [verified: [Apple support thread](https://discussions.apple.com/thread/254793035)]. GoodNotes has no ruler of its own; Notability's is the same two-finger pattern [unverified].
- GoodNotes Zoom Window: a fixed writing pad; a blue "auto-advance" area appears at the pad's left once a stroke crosses the pad's middle; writing in it moves the box forward; at the page edge the box moves down one line; the left margin is set with glyphs in the pad's top bar; Auto Advance is a setting [verified: snippets of [GoodNotes Zoom Window](https://support.goodnotes.com/hc/en-us/articles/7353756826383) and [feedback](https://feedback.goodnotes.com/forums/191274-customer-suggestions-for-goodnotes/suggestions/44989567-auto-advance-window-improvement)].

## Part C. The design chosen (built in this step; choices in DECISIONS)

1. **The Lasso owns the Pencil when there is ink.** While the Lasso tool is on, its Ink filter is on and the page has strokes, the ink pad catches the pointer (as it does for Pen, Highlighter, Eraser): the page text never sees the Pencil, the stylus touch is cancelled, and the page is made not editable for the length of the gesture (the Scribble cure penSelect already used). Words join a selection only as whole blocks inside the loop (the universal Lasso), like GoodNotes text boxes. On a page with no ink, or with the Ink filter off, nothing changes: a Pencil drag still selects text.
2. **Handles are solid.** Every handle (selection corners and turn, ruler, magnifier box) cancels the stylus touch, and a transform makes the page not editable while it lasts. The selection menu is "chrome" so Pencil taps on it are clicked by penTap like the bars.
3. **A tap outside deselects**, as GoodNotes does. A tap through the pad on words still counts for double and triple taps.
4. **Tap filter (`ink/tapFilter.ts`, tunable):** a stroke shorter than `TAP_MS` (90 ms) that travelled less than `TAP_PX` (4 px) is a tap, not ink. A press held longer is a deliberate dot. A second tap-like stroke at the same spot within `DOUBLE_MS` (350 ms) and `DOUBLE_PX` (24 px) is a double tap: the first mark (if one was kept) is taken back, and the double tap selects the word under it (triple: the line), with the mini toolbar.
5. **One tap counter.** `tapSelect.ts` counts taps for Lasso, Pen and Highlighter (through the pad too). `penSelect.ts` loses its own double-tap rule and no longer places a caret on pointer down: a Pencil drag starts selecting only once it has moved 4 px, so taps leave the selection alone.
6. **Ruler:** the body is a solid object. One pointer anywhere on it moves it; a second finger turns it about the first (two-finger twist); the stylus touch is cancelled on it; nothing reaches the text. The end handles still stretch and turn.
7. **Magnifier:** a first-use hint in the pad ("Write big here. It lands small in the box on the page. The box moves on by itself."), a stronger box with a tinted fill and a label, and the whole box draggable (not only the ⠿ handle).

Not adopted: GoodNotes' "touching counts as selected" (our half-inside rule stays; a tap on a stroke already selects it), Ink & Switch's clockwise rule, GoodNotes' blue auto-advance zone (our pad already advances when writing nears its right edge; a margin setting is an idea for later).
