Learning Notes – Privacy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Your notes stay on your device

Notes, pictures, recordings and books are stored in your browser on your own device. We do not run a server that can read them.

Sync is end-to-end encrypted

If you turn on Sync, your notes are encrypted on your device with your passphrase before they leave it. The sync database (Supabase) and your own Google Drive only ever hold locked data we cannot read. Sign-in with Google is used only to identify you. The app asks for access only to the Drive files it creates itself, in one folder.

AI is off unless you add your own key

If you add an AI key (for example Claude), the text you choose to send goes directly from your device to that provider. You see what will be sent before it is sent. Locked pages are never sent unless you allow it.

No tracking, no ads

The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. We do not sell or share data.

Deleting your data

Clear the site's data in your browser to remove everything from that device. To remove synced data, turn off Sync in Settings and delete the "Learning Notes (encrypted)" folder from your Google Drive.
