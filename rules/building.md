# Building a Step

- **Step start:** ask only the important questions, all together, each with a recommendation. Review IDEAS.md with the owner before each new step.
- **During:** decide small things yourself, log them in the README Decision Log, and don't stop.
- **Step end:** list the decisions you made so the owner can change them.
- Short replies. Risks, bugs and weak decisions are always said at once, in full.
- New ideas that come up mid-build go into IDEAS.md (date, short description, likely phase), not into the current step; just say "N new ideas saved".
- Small issues the owner finds during testing go into a polish list and are fixed in the next step.
- **Stop only for:** a blocker, a rule conflict, going over budget or the token cap, anything irreversible, or a big decision no rule covers.
- Commit after every working part. If the app stops, tell the owner how to restart it.
